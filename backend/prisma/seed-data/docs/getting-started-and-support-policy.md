# Getting started and support policy

## Support hours and channels
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST. Critical production incidents on the Business plan are handled 24/7. You can reach us through this support portal, the in-app assistant, or by email. Phone support is available for Business plan customers only.

## Response time targets
We prioritise tickets by impact. Critical tickets (production down, security issues, data loss) get a first response within 1 hour and a resolution target of 4 hours. High priority tickets get a first response within 4 hours. Medium priority tickets get a response within 1 business day, and low priority tickets within 3 business days.

## Plans
The Pro plan is for small teams of up to 10 members and includes email support. The Business plan supports unlimited members, SSO, audit logs, a 99.9% uptime SLA and priority support. Students and registered non-profits get 50% off any plan; apply from Settings > Billing with a valid institution email or registration certificate.

## Data residency and privacy
Customer data for Indian workspaces is stored in our Mumbai region data centre and backed up daily to a second Indian region. We never sell customer data. You can export or permanently delete your workspace data at any time from Settings > Workspace > Data.

## Feature requests
We review every feature request. Submit ideas through a support ticket with the category "Feature request" or vote on existing ideas in the community forum. Popular requests are reviewed by the product team every month and added to the public roadmap.

## Getting started
New workspaces include a guided setup checklist on the home page. Invite your team from Settings > Members, connect your first integration, and explore the sample data. Free onboarding webinars run every Tuesday and Thursday at 4:00 PM IST.
