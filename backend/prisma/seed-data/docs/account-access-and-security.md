# Account access and security

## Resetting your password
To reset your password, open the sign-in page and select "Forgot password". Enter the email address on your account and we will send a reset link. The link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once. If the email does not arrive within 5 minutes, check your spam folder and make sure the address matches the one on your account. Requesting a new link invalidates all previous links, so always use the most recent email.

## Locked accounts
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes to protect it from password-guessing attacks. You do not need to contact support: wait 15 minutes and sign in again, or reset your password to unlock the account immediately.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
You can enable 2FA from Settings > Security using any authenticator app. When you enable 2FA we show 10 single-use backup codes; store them somewhere safe. If you lose your phone, sign in with a backup code and then set up 2FA again on the new device. If you have lost both your phone and your backup codes, raise a support ticket from your registered email address; for your security, identity verification takes up to 2 business days.

## Single sign-on (SSO)
Business plan workspaces can sign in with Google or Microsoft SSO. If SSO fails with an "account not found" error, ask your workspace admin to confirm your email is invited to the workspace. SSO users cannot reset their password in our app; reset it with your identity provider instead.

## Changing your email address
Go to Settings > Profile, enter the new email address and confirm it with the verification link we send. The old address keeps working until the new one is verified.

## Suspicious activity
If you see sign-ins you do not recognise under Settings > Security > Active sessions, select "Sign out of all sessions" and reset your password immediately, then contact support so we can review the activity. Our team will never ask for your password or 2FA codes.
