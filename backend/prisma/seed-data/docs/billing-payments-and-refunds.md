# Billing, payments and refunds

## Billing cycle and invoices
Subscriptions are billed at the start of each billing period, monthly or yearly. Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and can be downloaded at any time from Settings > Billing > Invoices as PDF. To change the company name, address or GST number on future invoices, update Settings > Billing > Billing details before the next billing date. Already-issued invoices can be re-issued once by contacting support.

## Payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI and net banking. To update your card, open Settings > Billing > Payment method and select "Replace card". The new card is used from the next charge.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry automatically after 1, 3 and 5 days and email the billing contact each time. Your workspace stays active during the retry period. Common causes are insufficient balance, an expired card or a bank blocking international transactions.

## Double charges and pending payments
If you were charged twice for the same invoice, the duplicate charge is detected automatically and refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days; you do not need to raise a ticket unless it has been longer than 7 business days. UPI payments can show as pending for up to 48 hours. If the money was deducted but the payment is still pending after 48 hours, it is reversed by your bank automatically; if it is not, contact support with the UPI transaction reference (UTR number).

## Refund policy
New subscriptions can be cancelled for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Annual plans cancelled after 14 days receive a prorated refund for unused full months. Monthly plans are not refunded after 14 days, but you keep access until the end of the paid period. Approved refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear on your statement.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. To switch from monthly to yearly billing, open Settings > Billing > Plan and choose "Switch to yearly"; yearly billing includes a 20% discount.

## Promo codes
Promo codes can be applied at checkout or under Settings > Billing > Promo code before the next billing date. Codes cannot be combined and do not apply to past invoices.
